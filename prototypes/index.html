Democratising Data Centres

Five one-shot prototypes from the Excalidraw board. Same pipeline — news ingest, geoloc flags, dialogue, redesign, undisclosed sensemaking — five different objects. Each folder has a playable index.html and a PRD.md.

  1. 01 · civic journalism

    The Parish Ledger

    A county broadsheet. Grease-pencil flags, letters to the editor, a typesetter’s note of what the parish will stand on.

    PRD · 01-parish-ledger/PRD.md
  2. 02 · hydrographic survey

    Loadshed Atlas

    A harbor chart of compute. Campuses are wrecks, water is a sounding, agreement is a tide staff.

    PRD · 02-loadshed-atlas/PRD.md
  3. 03 · factory twin

    Compute Works

    A tin-litho marble works. Stamp a shipping tag, walk robots, read the punch-card that names the part that fails.

    PRD · 03-compute-works/PRD.md
  4. 04 · civic officiating

    The Referee

    A night booth between Operator and Parish. You write reasons. Yellow means a condition. Red abandons the fixture.

    PRD · 04-the-referee/PRD.md
  5. 05 · participatory gym

    Open Meeting, Article 19

    A high-school gym. Index cards, yarn consensus, foam-core site plans, a voice vote in the minutes.

    PRD · 05-town-meeting/PRD.md
